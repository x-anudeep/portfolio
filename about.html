About Me

Passionate about building reliable systems and user-friendly products at the intersection of full-stack development, test-driven engineering, and cloud technologies.

On the side, I’m building projects like a Student Management System (Java, Angular) for role-based student/course workflows and Admin Gate, a helpdesk with OTP auth and content management. These have sharpened my end-to-end development, security, and testing skills.
